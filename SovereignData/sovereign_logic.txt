Order Flow Imbalance (OFI):

Let:
Bid_i(t), Ask_i(t) = bid/ask sizes at level i
ΔBidPrice_i = BidPrice_i(t) - BidPrice_i(t-1)
ΔAskPrice_i = AskPrice_i(t) - AskPrice_i(t-1)

OFI_t = Σ_i [BidSize_i * 𝟙(ΔBidPrice_i ≥ 0)] − Σ_i [AskSize_i * 𝟙(ΔAskPrice_i ≤ 0)]

Alternative continuous approximation:
OFI_t ≈ Σ (ΔBidVolume - ΔAskVolume)

Interpretation:
OFI_t > 0 → buy-side dominance
OFI_t < 0 → sell-side dominance

Short-term price impact model:
ΔP_t = β · OFI_t + ε_t

β estimated via regression:
β = Cov(OFI, ΔP) / Var(OFI)

Microstructure assumption:
Market orders consume liquidity → imbalance precedes price move

Noise filtering:
OFI_smooth = EMA(OFI, α)
